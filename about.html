About Me

Who I Am

I am Andria Dididze, a young developer from Georgia with a strong interest in building for the web. I enjoy the moment when a few lines of code turn into something people can see, click and use.

I am at the beginning of my professional journey, and I approach every project with curiosity, patience and a willingness to learn.

What I Am Interested In

Front-End Development

I am most interested in the part of the web that people see and interact with. I care about clean structure, readable code, accessibility and a smooth user experience.

Learning New Technologies

Web technologies change quickly, and I enjoy keeping up. I regularly explore new tools, frameworks and best practices to grow my skills.

Building Practical Projects

I learn best by building. I prefer creating projects that solve real problems, such as price comparison, online shopping and business management, rather than only following tutorials.
